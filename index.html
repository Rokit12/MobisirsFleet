How to sign in

  1. Get your accountYour administrator creates your username and gives you a temporary password.
  2. Sign inEnter them in the form. Usernames are not case-sensitive.
  3. Set your own passwordOn first sign-in you choose a new password of at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  4. Forgot it?Ask an administrator to reset it from Users & Access.
